Product Positioning

SecureOne is an AppSec operations platform.

SecureOne is the place your team manages AppSec work: run scans, review findings, apply policy gates, and track remediation in one workflow.

Deployment Models

SaaS

Fully managed hosted dashboard for quick onboarding.

Self-Hosted

Deploy in your own environment when you need strict infrastructure and data control.

MSSP/Team Use

Manage multiple projects and clients from a single operational view.

What SecureOne Does

Orchestrates Testing

Coordinates SAST, SCA, secrets, and DAST workflows.

Tracks Findings

Keeps vulnerability status, severity, and ownership clear for engineering and security.

Enforces Policy

Applies CI/CD gates based on severity thresholds your team defines.

Supports Retesting

Validation cycle for remediation closure and final assurance.

How It Connects to Services

BountyBreach offensive security services produce validated findings and remediation guidance. SecureOne helps teams operationalize those findings continuously in development pipelines.
